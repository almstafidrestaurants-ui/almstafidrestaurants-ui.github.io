Restaurant

Crepe Time

A photo-led dessert menu with separate sections for crêpes, waffles and mini pancakes.

Visit website GitHub repository

What visitors can explore

The menu keeps dessert formats in separate sections and shows variations such as Nutella, Lotus and pistachio within those sections. That makes flavor comparison easier while preserving the distinction between formats.

How the site is organized

Product photography helps visitors distinguish similar items. On a phone, the category structure provides a shorter way back to the rest of the menu after browsing a long list.
