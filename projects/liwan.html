LIWAN Restaurant

A restaurant website that introduces the dining experience and gives guests a direct route to the menu.

Visit website

What visitors can explore

The welcome imagery establishes the restaurant’s atmosphere before guests start browsing. A digital menu then serves a different purpose: it lets diners look through the offering on their phones at their own pace.

How the site is organized

The design priority is to keep the welcome screen and the menu distinct. Guests should be able to identify the restaurant quickly and reach the information they came for with minimal effort.
