Yalla Shop

An Arabic-first catalog for everyday shopping, from fresh produce and groceries to stationery and cleaning products.

Visit website GitHub repository

What visitors can explore

The right-to-left storefront starts with broad shopping departments so visitors can identify the type of product they need before viewing individual items. Category images support the Arabic labels when scanning the page on a phone.

How the site is organized

A mixed catalog has different browsing needs from a single-brand shop. Separating departments gives food, household supplies and stationery a clear place without forcing them into one continuous list.
