BYS Cosmetics

A beauty storefront with category-led browsing and distinct paths for shoppers, consultants and wholesale customers.

Visit website

What visitors can explore

The storefront introduces the BYS range with a visual catalog. Visitors can start by browsing categories and use the customer, consultant or wholesale path when they need to identify their shopping role.

How the site is organized

Beauty products benefit from imagery that keeps packaging recognizable. The catalog and role selection solve two separate tasks: finding a product and choosing how to engage with the business.
